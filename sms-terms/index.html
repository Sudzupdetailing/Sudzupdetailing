Terms for the Sudz Up Detailing LLC text messaging program. Last updated 2026-08-26.

1. Program description

Sudz Up Detailing LLC operates a text messaging program for customers and prospective customers in Hartford, Wisconsin and the surrounding area. By opting in, you agree to receive text messages from us at the mobile number you provided.

Messages relate to auto detailing services and fall into these categories:

  • Appointment messages — booking confirmations, reminders, and changes to a scheduled time.
  • Service messages — quotes, questions about your vehicle, and notification that your vehicle is ready.
  • Promotional messages — seasonal offers and availability. These are sent only to customers who have separately agreed to receive them.

2. How to opt in

You can join the program by:

  • Ticking the text message consent box on our booking page. The box is unticked by default.
  • Ticking the consent box when booking through our online booking system.
  • Giving us your mobile number in person, by phone or on a paper form and agreeing to be contacted by text.
  • Texting us first at 414-286-1609. This allows us to reply to that conversation. It does not by itself enrol you in promotional messages.

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services from us.

3. Message frequency

Message frequency varies and depends on your interaction with us. Most customers receive only a small number of messages around a single booking. Promotional messages, where you have agreed to them, are infrequent.

4. Cost

Message and data rates may apply. These are charged by your mobile carrier under your own plan, not by us. Check with your carrier if you are unsure of your rates.

5. How to opt out — reply STOP

You can cancel at any time by replying STOP to any message from us. You will receive a single confirmation that you have been unsubscribed, after which we will send you no further messages.

You may also opt out by calling 414-286-1609 or emailing gio@sudzupdetail.com and asking to be removed. To rejoin, reply START or contact us and ask to be added back.

6. How to get help — reply HELP

Reply HELP to any message from us for assistance. You can also reach us directly:

  • Phone or text: 414-286-1609
  • Email: gio@sudzupdetail.com
  • Address: 2948 WI-83, Hartford, WI 53027

You must be at least 18 years old and the account holder of, or authorised to use, the mobile number you provide. If your mobile number changes or is reassigned, please tell us so we do not message someone who has not consented.
