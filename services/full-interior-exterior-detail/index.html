Everything, in the right order

The VIP Clean includes the complete interior vacuum, vinyl, rubber and plastic treatment, spot stain removal, door jambs and glass — then adds the exterior hand wash and polish, wheels cleaned and tires shined.

Doing both together is not just a bundle for convenience. It is genuinely better work, because sequence matters. Wheels and lower panels are the dirtiest part of any vehicle, and doing them in the same session as the interior means we are not opening clean doors with dirty hands or letting arch grit find its way back into a freshly cleaned sill.

Door jambs are the clearest example. A jamb sits between the interior and exterior worlds. Cleaning the cabin but not the jamb leaves an obvious dirty frame around otherwise clean work, and it is the first thing anyone notices when they open the door.

Who should book this rather than the Quick Clean

There are four situations where the VIP is clearly the right call, and we would rather tell you which one you are in than upsell by default.

You are selling the vehicle. Full stop — book the VIP, and do it before you take photographs.

You are coming out of a Wisconsin winter. Salt has been in the jambs, arches and carpets for months, and doing only the interior leaves the corrosive half of the problem untouched.

You have not detailed the vehicle in over a year. At that point there is exterior contamination that a wash alone will not shift, and the polish step is doing real work.

You are driving a distance to reach us. If you are coming from Kewaskum or Germantown, splitting the work over two visits is a poor use of your time.

If none of those apply and the vehicle is in decent shape outside, the Quick Clean is probably enough and we will say so.

What a full detail cannot do

We would rather be clear about the ceiling than have you disappointed at collection.

A detail does not repair. Cracked dash tops, torn seat fabric, sagging headliners, worn-through bolsters, stone chips and clear coat that has already failed are all beyond it. Deep scratches remain deep scratches. A cigarette smoke odour that has permeated a headliner over years can be substantially reduced but rarely eliminated entirely.

What a full detail does is bring everything that is still structurally sound back to as good as it can look. On most vehicles between three and twelve years old, that gap is much bigger than the owner expects — which is why the reaction at collection is usually surprise rather than mild satisfaction.

Timing and preparation

The VIP takes meaningfully longer than the Quick Clean. Vehicle size and condition drive it, so we will give you a realistic window when you book rather than a number that sounds good on the phone.

Two things help enormously. First, take personal belongings out beforehand — we work faster and you are not relying on us to decide what is rubbish and what matters. Second, tell us about any specific problem: a spill you know about, a smell, a stain you have already tried to treat yourself. Products previously applied to a stain change how we approach it, and knowing saves us both time.
