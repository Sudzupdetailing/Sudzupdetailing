Work trucks deserve a proper detail

A lot of what comes to us from Allenton is a working pickup. There is a persistent idea that a work truck is not worth detailing because it will just get dirty again. We would push back on that gently. A truck that is your workplace for fifty hours a week is exactly the vehicle where a clean cabin makes a daily difference, and it is usually the one carrying the most abrasive material.

Work truck interiors have a distinct profile. Dried mud in the driver's footwell in layers. Fine agricultural or construction dust across the dash and in every vent. Tool marks and grease on the seat bolster where you slide in. Sawdust or feed in the seat seams. A rubber floor mat that has never been lifted.

The single most valuable thing we do on a work truck is get under and behind things. Sliding the seats fully forward and back to reach the rails, lifting mats to clean the carpet or vinyl underneath, and clearing the vents with compressed air. That is where the material actually is.

Farm dust is abrasive and persistent

The fields around Allenton generate serious dust at planting and harvest. Agricultural dust is different from road dust because it carries organic matter, which means it holds moisture and can start to smell in a warm cabin.

It also gets everywhere. Dust drawn in through the ventilation system settles behind the dash vents and in the blower housing, so it re-emerges every time you turn the fan on. We clear what is reachable, but if your cabin air filter has not been changed in years, that is worth doing at the same time. We will tell you if it looks like the cause rather than pretend the interior clean alone will fix it.

US 41 runs right past, so Allenton vehicles get highway film on top of the farm dust. Same split-personality issue as Richfield: the top half and the bottom half of the vehicle need different attention.

Straight pricing for work vehicles

Trucks are $150 for the Sudz Quick Clean and $250 for the full Sudz Up VIP Clean. If a vehicle is genuinely heavily soiled we will say so before we start and give you a revised figure, rather than quoting a low number and adjusting it at collection.

For a work truck that gets used hard, the Quick Clean two or three times a year is often better value than one big annual detail. It keeps material from building up to the point where it needs extraction.
