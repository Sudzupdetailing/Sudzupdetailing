Service Area

Where We Detail

Twelve communities across Washington and Dodge County. Each page below covers what we actually see in vehicles from that area — the roads, the seasons and the specific problems — rather than the same paragraph with the town name swapped.

Washington County

Hartford, WI

We are based on WI-83 in Hartford. This is the town we work in every day, and it is the one place where we can usually fit a vehicle in on short notice.

 Washington County

West Bend, WI

West Bend is the largest community we serve and about a twenty minute run from our shop in Hartford. It is worth the drive, and here is what we typically find in a West Bend vehicle.

 Washington County

Slinger, WI

Slinger is about ten minutes east of our shop. Between the Speedway and Little Switzerland, it produces two of the most distinctive vehicle messes in the county.

 Washington County

Germantown, WI

Germantown sits on the busiest highway corridor in the county. That shapes almost everything about how the vehicles we see from there wear.

 Washington County

Richfield, WI

Richfield kept its rural character while growing to nearly twelve thousand residents. The mix of gravel side roads and highway commuting gives its vehicles a split personality.

 Washington County

Jackson, WI

Jackson is one of the fastest growing communities in Washington County, about seven miles south of West Bend. Growing communities mean family vehicles, and family vehicles mean interiors.

 Washington County

Kewaskum, WI

Kewaskum calls itself the gateway to the Kettle Moraine, and that is exactly the problem. Recreation vehicles come back dirtier than commuter vehicles, every time.

 Washington County

Allenton, WI

Allenton is a small community on the US 41 corridor north of Hartford, surrounded by working farmland. Work vehicles outnumber show vehicles here, and we detail accordingly.

 Washington County

Colgate, WI

Colgate is a small unincorporated community in the south of the county, sharing the 53017 ZIP with Richfield. Rural, quiet, and hard on the lower half of a vehicle.

 Washington County

Erin, WI

Erin is rural Washington County at its most scenic, and it is home to Erin Hills, the public course that hosted the 2017 U.S. Open. Golf traffic gives its vehicles a specific signature.

 Dodge County

Rubicon, WI

Rubicon is just over the line into Dodge County, west of Hartford. It is farm country, and farm country vehicles have needs that a car wash does not address.

 Dodge County

Neosho, WI

Neosho is a small Dodge County village west of Hartford. There is not much in the way of detailing options out that way, which is exactly why we list it.
