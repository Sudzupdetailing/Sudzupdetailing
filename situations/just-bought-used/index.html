Just Bought a Used Car

You do not know who was in it or what they did in it. One full detail at the start gives you a car whose condition you actually know, and it usually finds a few things.

What a sale clean hides

A car being sold has almost always had a surface clean and something fragranced applied. Both genuinely work for a week or two. Then the fragrance fades, the car warms up in the sun, and whatever was underneath starts off-gassing again.

That is the mechanism behind the used-car smell that appears a fortnight after purchase. It was not there when you bought it, in the sense that you could not smell it. It was there in every other sense. A full detail in the first week takes the car back to what it actually is, and it is far better to know that in week one than in month three.

What we find

The cabin air filter is almost never changed before a sale, and it is a reservoir of the previous owner's air. The spare wheel well is almost never cleaned, and it holds whatever leaked through the boot carpet over the car's life. Under-seat storage and the gaps around seat bases hold what the previous owner dropped.

We also find the things a buyer's inspection misses: a damp patch under a mat that says a seal leaks, a headliner that says the car was smoked in, a repainted panel that behaves differently under clay. None of that changes whether you bought a good car. All of it is worth knowing.

Making it yours

There is a practical value and there is a less tangible one. The practical value is a known baseline — you know what the interior smells like, what the paint condition is under the contamination, and what was in the corners. Anything that appears after that is new.

The other value is that a car that has been fully reset feels like yours rather than someone else's. That sounds soft and it is not. It changes how you treat it.

Protection at the start

A used car in its first week is the second-best time to protect it, after a new car on day one. The interior is as clean as it is going to be; the paint has just been decontaminated. Fabric and leather protection and a sealant or coating on the paint go on to a clean surface, which is the only way they work properly, and they mean the next owner's problems start from a better place.
